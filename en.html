ABOUT

Inspired by Mio.
Creating worlds.

Curiosity about the cosmos,
brought to life through imagination.

Students at Tokyo Zokei University created works inspired by Mio, Mercury, planetary exploration and space science. As they deepened their understanding of science, they explored the wonder of space through their own perspectives, using film and interactive experiences.

Mio is JAXA’s spacecraft for studying Mercury’s magnetic field and magnetosphere. It is part of BepiColombo, a joint Japanese–European mission to Mercury.

On September 11, 2026, Associate Professor Makoto Hosoya, with the cooperation of EDENA Co., Ltd., organized a field visit to the Space Science Exploration Exhibition Building at JAXA Sagamihara Campus and the Institute of Space and Astronautical Science. The visit was part of an investigation into creating exhibits about space science exploration. Students are putting their findings into practice through exhibits on related themes.
